📋 Pool Rules

🟢 Group Stage

  • 1 pt per real match point (win=3, draw=1)
  • 1 pt for advancing as best 3rd place
  • 3 pts for finishing 2nd in group
  • 5 pts for winning the group (1st place)

🔵 Knockout Bonuses

  • 5 pts - Round of 16
  • 10 pts - Quarterfinals
  • 15 pts - Semifinals
  • 20 pts - Final
  • 30 pts - Champions 🏆

✖ Tier Multipliers

  • Tier 1 - 1x (Spain, France, England)
  • Tier 2 - 1.5x (Brazil, Portugal, Argentina, Germany)
  • Tier 3 - 2x (Netherlands, Norway, Belgium, Morocco)
  • Tier 4 - 2.5x (Colombia, Mexico, Switzerland, USA)
  • Tier 5 - 3x (Senegal, Japan, Uruguay, Ecuador, Turkiye, Croatia)
  • Tier 6 - 4x (Sweden, Austria, Canada, Scotland)
  • Tier 7 - 5x (Cote d'Ivoire, Czechia, South Korea, Egypt, Ghana)
  • Tier 8 - 5.5x (Paraguay, Algeria, Bosnia, Tunisia, Australia)
  • Tier 9 - 6x (Iran, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Cape Verde, DR Congo)
  • Tier 10 - 8x (Qatar, Uzbekistan, New Zealand, Panama)
  • Tier 11 - 10x (Curacao, Jordan, Haiti, Iraq)

⭐ Special Rules

  • Double Points team = 2x their tier multiplier for all points
  • Golden Boot correct pick = 10 bonus pts (no multiplier)
  • Tiebreaker: youngest goalscorer age in days - closest without going under
