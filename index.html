📑 Pool Rules

Group Stage Scoring

  • 1 point for every match point earned (Win=3pts, Draw=1pt)
  • +1 point for advancing as best 3rd place team
  • +3 points for finishing 2nd in group
  • +5 points for winning the group

Knockout Round Bonuses (all multiplied by tier)

  • Round of 16: +5 pts  |  Quarterfinals: +10 pts  |  Semifinals: +15 pts
  • Final: +20 pts  |  Tournament Winner: +30 pts

Tier Multipliers

  • Tier 1 (1×): Spain, France, England
  • Tier 2 (1.5×): Brazil, Portugal, Argentina, Germany
  • Tier 3 (2×): Netherlands, Norway, Belgium, Morocco
  • Tier 4 (2.5×): Colombia, Mexico, Switzerland, United States
  • Tier 5 (3×): Senegal, Japan, Uruguay, Ecuador, Turkiye, Croatia
  • Tier 6 (4×): Sweden, Austria, Canada, Scotland
  • Tier 7 (5×): Cote D'Ivoire, Czechia, South Korea, Egypt, Ghana
  • Tier 8 (5.5×): Paraguay, Algeria, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Tunisia, Australia
  • Tier 9 (6×): Iran, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Cape Verde, DR Congo
  • Tier 10 (8×): Qatar, Uzbekistan, New Zealand, Panama
  • Tier 11 (10×): Curacao, Jordan, Haiti, Iraq

Special Rules

  • Double Points Team: One team already in your tier picks — doubles all multiplied points for that team throughout the tournament.
  • Golden Boot: Correct top scorer = 10 flat points (no multiplier).
  • Tiebreaker: Age in days of youngest goalscorer. Closest without going under wins.
